Scale & tokens

Back link

Full-page screens that sit under a section return with one link on its own row above the content — arrow glyph at 16px, label at 14px, muted ink brightening to pine on hover. It replaces a breadcrumb; never both.

Filter bar

Filter and toolbar controls are their own button: surface fill, hairline border, 10px/15px, radius 9 — lighter than a secondary action so a row of them reads as one instrument. A trailing caret when it opens a menu; an active filter carries a pine count pill.

Checkbox

18px square, radius 5. Unchecked is a 1.5px muted border on nothing; checked fills pine with a dark tick. Selection is always pine — brass is an accent colour, never a state. The whole label is the hit target.

Search field

A standard field with a search glyph inset left and text starting at 40px. Same border, focus ring and height as every other input — the glyph is the only difference.

Chip input

Tags and other free lists. The field is one bordered box; each value is a pill on tertiary with a × at 8px gap, and the caret input flexes to fill the last row. Enter commits, × removes — no Add button.

Tabs

In-page section switch: 24px gap, 12px bottom padding, a 2px pine underline on the active one and a hairline under the row. For switching what a page shows — never for navigation between screens (that is the rail) and never for prototype states (that is the dev bar).

Split button

A primary action with variants behind a caret. Pine solid body, caret on the darker pine with a hairline between; the pair shares one 11px radius and one pine shadow. Only where a secondary variant genuinely exists (submit and submit-and-notify).

Anchored action bar

When a page scrolls its own pane, the commit actions leave the scroll and sit at the foot of the frame, divided by a hairline: the escape hatch on the left, the primary on the right. It never floats over content and never scrolls out of reach.

Calendar grid

One hour is 48px, so a full day is 1176px and the pane scrolls rather than the page. Hour labels sit in a 56px mono rail, gridlines are drawn as a repeating gradient (47px page + 1px sunken), and entries inset 4px so adjacent days never touch.

Form dirty state

A long form says whether it owes you a save. Untouched: the primary is disabled and names the reason. Touched: a brass pill appears beside the title, the primary lights up, and the escape hatch becomes Discard changes. Saving confirms with a toast, never silence.

Date picker

Opens from the period stepper’s label, never from an icon of its own. Days are mono on a 7-column grid; the picked week reads as one band — pine at 28% across it, solid pine on the first and last day — and today carries an inset pine ring. Out-of-month days stay visible but disabled-dim so the grid never reflows.
